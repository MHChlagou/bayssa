## English

**Subject: Licensing clarification for the Arabic hadith content, version 1.8.0, in a free offline web app**

Assalamu alaykum wa rahmatullah,

Thank you for the Encyclopedia of Translated Prophetic Hadiths and for making it freely available.

I am [full name], and I am working on Nafissa, a free web app in Arabic for daily adhkar and duas. It has no advertising, no accounts and no tracking, and it works offline. It shows individual supplications, each with its source.

We are considering your Arabic content as the source of the wording and grading of prophetic supplications in the app. We have read the Terms and Policies on your site and want to be sure our use is within them before we use anything. Nothing from your site is in the app today.

We would be grateful for your answers to the following.

**1. Scope of the terms**

Your terms say that the contents of the translations may be downloaded and re-published on seven conditions. Do they also cover the Arabic hadith text, the grades and the takhrij in the Arabic download?

**2. Redistribution in a public web app**

May we show your Arabic hadith content in a public progressive web app, free of charge, hosted on GitHub Pages?

**3. The exact edition and version**

We would use the Arabic download whose header reads "Last update: 2026-10-14 (v1.8.0)". May we redistribute that edition as it is, and state that version in the app? Where should we follow new versions so that we update as your sixth condition asks?

**4. Attribution**

What wording do you prefer for naming the publisher and the source? We propose: "From the Encyclopedia of Translated Prophetic Hadiths, HadeethEnc.com, version 1.8.0", shown with each supplication and in the app's sources screen. Is a link to the hadith's page on your site required or welcome?

**5. Modification, vocalization and excerpting**

Your first condition forbids modification, addition and deletion. Our app shows supplications, so we would need to know whether each of these is permitted:

- showing only the words of the supplication from within a hadith, without the narrator's introduction and without the explanation, with the full hadith one tap away or linked;
- showing the hadith text with its grade and takhrij, and leaving out the explanation, word meanings and benefits;
- selecting some hadiths and not others;
- any change to vocalization or punctuation. We do not intend to make any, and would show your text exactly as published. We ask only so that the answer is on record.

If excerpting is not permitted, we would show the hadith in full as you publish it.

**6. Commercial use, offline distribution and public repositories**

- Our app is not commercial. Do your terms restrict commercial use by others? This matters because the app's source code will be published under an open-source licence. We keep the religious content outside that licence and would state your terms beside it, but anyone could download the repository.
- The app stores its content on the user's device so that it works offline. Is that offline copy acceptable?
- May the data file containing your content be kept in a public GitHub repository, with your notice and version information kept inside it as your fourth condition asks?

**7. Corrections**

If we or our users notice something in the text, where should we report it?

We would be glad to send you a preview of the app and the list of hadiths we would use before anything is published.

Jazakum Allahu khayran.

هل يجوز لنا عرض محتواكم العربي في تطبيق ويب عام مجاني مستضاف على GitHub Pages؟

**3. الطبعة والإصدار بعينهما**

نود استعمال ملف التنزيل العربي الذي في رأسه «Last update: 2026-10-14 (v1.8.0)». فهل يجوز لنا إعادة نشر هذا الإصدار كما هو، مع ذكر رقمه في التطبيق؟ وأين نتابع الإصدارات الجديدة لنحدّث ما عندنا كما يطلب شرطكم السادس؟

**4. النسبة**

ما الصيغة التي تفضلونها في الإشارة إلى الناشر والمصدر؟ نقترح: «من موسوعة الأحاديث النبوية، HadeethEnc.com، الإصدار 1.8.0»، تُعرض مع كل دعاء وفي صفحة المصادر في التطبيق. وهل يلزم، أو يحسن، وضع رابط صفحة الحديث في موقعكم؟

**5. التعديل والتشكيل والاقتطاع**

شرطكم الأول يمنع التعديل والإضافة والحذف. وتطبيقنا يعرض أدعية، فنحتاج إلى معرفة حكم كل مما يأتي:

- عرض لفظ الدعاء وحده من داخل الحديث، دون مقدمة الراوي ودون الشرح، مع إتاحة الحديث كاملاً بضغطة أو برابط؛
- عرض نص الحديث مع درجته وتخريجه، وترك الشرح ومعاني الكلمات والفوائد؛
- اختيار بعض الأحاديث دون بعض؛
- أي تغيير في التشكيل أو علامات الترقيم. لا ننوي شيئاً من ذلك، وسنعرض نصكم كما نشرتموه، وإنما نسأل ليكون الجواب مثبتاً.

وإن كان الاقتطاع غير مأذون فيه فسنعرض الحديث كاملاً كما تنشرونه.
